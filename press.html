One-liner

Copple is the AI-native operating system for independent coaches — scheduling, video, transcripts, insights, follow-ups, and invoicing in one workspace.

Short bio

Copple replaces Calendly, Zoom, Notion, and Stripe for coaches. Its AI extracts breakthroughs and action items from every session, drafts follow-up emails in the coach's voice, and handles invoicing — without the coach lifting a finger. Built by Zyppr, a remote team across Dubai, Bangalore, and London.
