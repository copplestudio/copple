Copple isn't a meeting recorder with a transcription bolted on. It is purpose-built for the rituals of premium coaching — synthesis, follow-up, timeline, pulse — every detail tuned to feel invisible in the work and indispensable around it.

01 — Synthesis

Beyond a transcript.
Toward a thesis.

Our coaching-tuned LLM doesn't summarize. It analyzes the structure of the conversation, surfaces the moment of breakthrough, and maps the arc from this session to the last.

  • Golden quote extraction

    The single sentence that names what the client couldn't, until they did.

  • Sentiment trajectory

    Mapped from frantic → clarity, with timestamps and the words that turned it.

  • Talk ratio & pacing

    Coach-to-client speech share with words-per-minute over time.

02 — Drafts

Follow-ups that sound like you.

Spend zero minutes writing post-session recaps. Copple drafts an empathetic, tone-matched email from the breakthroughs and agreed actions — ready to refine and send from your own inbox.

  • Voice-matched copy

    Trained on a small sample of your past emails — never across coaches.

  • Inline editing

    Refine before send; Copple learns from your edits, session over session.

  • Send from your inbox

    Native Gmail and Outlook integration — replies thread normally.

03 — Timelines

Continuity, visualized.

Stop losing context between sessions. Copple groups conversations into packages, so a 3, 6, or 12-month engagement reads as a single, evolving narrative.

  • Visual progress bar

    See how far in, what's next, what shifted between sessions.

  • Context recall

    Pull a quote, a goal, or a turning point from any past session in seconds.

  • Renewal alerts

    Know when a package is ending — and why a client is ready to re-up.

04 — Pulse

Catch the dip.
Before it churns.

Copple watches engagement patterns across the package — sentiment, talk ratios, action follow-through — and surfaces the early signals that a relationship needs attention.

  • Resonance score

    A weighted index of breakthroughs, follow-through, and emotional arc.

  • Risk indicators

    Quiet flags when patterns suggest a client is drifting — never alarmist.

  • Cohort benchmarks

    Compare against your own history, never against other coaches' clients.
