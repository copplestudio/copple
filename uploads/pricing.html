Frequently Asked Questions

Do you record video?

No. To ensure maximum privacy and client comfort, Copple's bot only records and processes audio. The audio is immediately transcribed and then permanently deleted from our servers.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no long-term contracts. You can cancel your subscription at any time from your billing dashboard.

Is my client data safe?

Security is our top priority. All transcripts and insights are encrypted at rest and in transit. We are fully GDPR and SOC2 compliant, and we never use your data to train public AI models.
